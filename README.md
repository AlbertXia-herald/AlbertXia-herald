## Selected Research

### TF-SepDualNet — Domain Adaptation for Acoustic Scene Classification

Research implementation developed during the **XJTLU SURF 2025** project *Data-Efficient and Low-Complexity Acoustic Scene Classification*.

The project investigates device and city domain shifts in acoustic scene classification using a shared time-frequency representation, dual scene-classification heads, and domain-adversarial learning.

**Highlights:** domain adaptation · time-frequency modeling · dual-head classification · PyTorch

[View repository →](https://github.com/AlbertXia-herald/TF-SepDualnet-used-in-acoustic-scene-classification)

---

### PrimeK-Net — Speech Enhancement & Phase Analysis

Previous research work based on **PrimeK-Net** for single-channel speech enhancement, including strict baseline reproduction and subsequent phase-oriented analysis.

The study examined phase concentration and statistical phase modeling as possible directions for improving phase-aware speech enhancement.

**Topics:** speech enhancement · baseline reproduction · spectral learning · phase analysis

---

### Audio Detection / Sound Event Detection

Current research preparation for my Final Year Project focuses on **audio detection and sound event detection**, including baseline systems, pretrained audio representations, ATST-based approaches, and two-stage training strategies.

The goal is to identify a well-grounded model-improvement direction while maintaining compatibility with established detection baselines.
